# Returns Policy (v3)

Effective date: 1 July 2025. Owner: Customer Operations.

## Return window

- **India:** the return window was reduced from 30 days to **14 days** for all orders placed on
  or after 1 July 2025. Orders placed before that date keep the 30-day window.
- **United Kingdom, Germany:** 30 days (unchanged, required by consumer law).
- **United States, Singapore:** 30 days.

## Conditions

Items must be unworn, with original tags. Shoes must be returned in the original box.
Accessories marked "final sale" cannot be returned.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days of the item reaching
the warehouse. Orders with status `returned` in the order system have been fully refunded.

## Why we changed it

Return rates in India rose in the first half of 2025 and reverse-logistics costs grew faster
than revenue. The shorter window is expected to lower the return rate, with some risk to
conversion on the web channel. Customer Operations will review the impact after Q3 2025.
